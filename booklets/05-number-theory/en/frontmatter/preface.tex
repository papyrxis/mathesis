Number theory takes the integers --- the most familiar objects
in all of mathematics --- and asks what happens when you actually look
closely: divisibility, modular arithmetic, the Euclidean algorithm,
the Chinese Remainder Theorem, Fermat and Euler's theorems, primality
testing, factorization, quadratic residues, and elliptic curves.

It leans on Booklet~04 for the ring and field language modular
arithmetic is really an instance of. It's also the most directly
useful booklet so far if you write software: primality testing and
elliptic curves are not abstract detours here, they're the two ideas
most of modern cryptography is built on.
