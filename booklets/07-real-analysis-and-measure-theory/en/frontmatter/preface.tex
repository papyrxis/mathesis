This is where calculus gets put back on rigorous footing.
Everyone learns limits, derivatives, and integrals informally first;
this booklet goes back and asks what a real number actually is, what a
limit actually means, and why Riemann integration eventually runs out
of room --- which is what Lebesgue integration and measure theory are
there to fix.

It depends on Booklet~02's construction of sets and cardinality more
than it looks like it should. It's also one of the most-depended-on
booklets in the whole project: complex analysis, topology, probability,
and functional analysis all assume this material rather than re-deriving
it.
