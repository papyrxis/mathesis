Complex analysis asks what changes once $\sqrt{-1}$ is allowed
to be a number like any other --- and the honest answer is: almost
everything gets \textit{better}. This booklet covers analytic functions
and the Cauchy-Riemann equations, contour integration, Laurent series
and residues, conformal mappings, Fourier series and the Fourier
transform, and then uses all of it on analytic number theory, the Prime
Number Theorem, and the Riemann zeta function.

It depends heavily on Booklet~07 for its notion of limits and
continuity. The zeta function chapter is the current horizon of this
booklet --- the Riemann Hypothesis shows up as the open problem it
actually is, not as a curiosity mentioned in passing.
