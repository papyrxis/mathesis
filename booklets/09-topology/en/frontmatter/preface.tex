Topology strips away distance and asks what's left: which
properties of a space survive being stretched, bent, or continuously
deformed, but not torn or glued. This booklet moves from metric and
topological spaces through compactness, connectedness, separation
axioms, the fundamental group and covering spaces, homology and
cohomology, manifolds, knot theory, fixed-point theorems, computational
topology, and homotopy type theory.

It depends on Booklet~07 for the metric-space intuition it immediately
generalizes past, and it's a direct prerequisite for differential
geometry, which needs manifolds before it can put curvature on them.
