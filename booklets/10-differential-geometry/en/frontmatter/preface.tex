Differential geometry is what you get when you insist that
curvature --- the thing that makes a sphere different from a plane ---
be defined precisely enough to compute with, not just gestured at. This
booklet covers smooth manifolds and tangent spaces, differential forms
and exterior calculus, integration on manifolds and Stokes' theorem,
Riemannian metrics and curvature, geodesics, Lie groups and Lie
algebras, and fiber bundles.

It depends on Booklet~09 for the manifolds themselves and Booklet~03
for the linear algebra every tangent space is quietly built from. This
is also, not coincidentally, the mathematical setting general
relativity is written in.
