Functional analysis asks what happens to linear algebra ---
Booklet~03's vector spaces, linear maps, eigenvalues --- once the spaces
are allowed to be infinite-dimensional. This booklet covers normed and
Banach spaces, Hilbert spaces, linear operators and the fundamental
theorems, spectral theory of bounded operators, compact operators and
Fredholm theory, operator algebras, distribution theory, and spectral
graph theory.

It depends on Booklet~03 for the linear algebra it's generalizing and
Booklet~07 for the analysis that generalization needs to stay rigorous.
Spectral graph theory, the closing chapter, is where this booklet loops
back to something concrete: graphs, from Booklet~06, analyzed with the
tools built here.
