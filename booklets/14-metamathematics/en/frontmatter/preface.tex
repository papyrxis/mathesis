Metamathematics turns the machinery from Booklet~01 back on
mathematics itself: instead of using logic and proof to establish facts
\textit{about} numbers or spaces, this booklet uses them to ask what
proof and mathematical truth even are. It covers Frege's foundations,
Russell's paradox, Principia Mathematica, Hilbert's program, Gödel's
completeness and incompleteness theorems, model theory, proof theory
and cut elimination, reverse mathematics, large cardinals, and
alternative foundations including homotopy type theory.

This is the closing booklet of \textit{Mathesis} for a reason: Gödel's
incompleteness theorems are, as honestly as I can put it, the reason I
stopped assuming mathematics was a closed, fully-decidable system
before I'd properly understood why it isn't.
