\markboth{About Mathesis}{About Mathesis}

\textit{Mathesis} is an open, ongoing exploration of mathematics from
first principles --- logic, sets, algebra, analysis, and onward ---
written the way the ideas are actually being learned rather than
polished into a textbook after the fact. It's released one booklet per
branch of the subject, and each booklet is updated as the corresponding
chapters go from outline to full write-up.

It sits alongside two companion projects by the same author: \textit{Arliz},
on data and computation from the transistor up, and \textit{The Art of
Algorithmic Analysis}, on analyzing algorithms rigorously. The three can
be read independently, but together they trace a path from ``what is a
number?'' to ``how do we build efficient software?''

This booklet is a self-contained snapshot of \BookletSubtitle\ and can be
read on its own. The complete, continuously updated project --- including
booklets not yet written on the day this edition was produced --- lives
at:

\begin{center}
  \url{https://github.com/papyrxis/mathesis}
\end{center}

Issues and pull requests are welcome. Corrections, in particular, are
worth more than they might seem: this is a document written by someone
still learning the material, and it improves through the people who
point out what's wrong, unclear, or missing.
